Whisper

A joystick, four directions, and a press. Whisper reads the smallest movement a person can still make and turns it into a full sentence, spoken out loud — and it gets better every time it guesses wrong.

Open the speaker →

Runs with no hardware attached — arrow keys drive the same loop.

01

Nudge

One residual movement — a thumb, a chin, whatever still works — steps through eight options laid out the way the joystick is.

02

Predict

Profile, time of day and recent phrases narrow everything a person might want to say down to the eight they probably mean right now.

03

Speak

A press expands the chosen intent into a natural sentence in their own register, and the browser says it aloud.

04

Learn

A wrong guess, corrected with a single nudge, is written back and reshapes what comes next. Every rejection is training data.

Built for people whose voice outlasts their movement.
